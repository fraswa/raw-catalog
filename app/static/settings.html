PREVIEWS

Full previews

Large JPEG previews have an independent path, maximum edge size, and JPEG quality. Missing previews can also be generated automatically when a photo is opened.

Examples: /storage/previews, /storage/photo-cache/previews, or /data/cache/previews.

Changing size or quality affects newly generated and on-demand previews. Use Rebuild previews to apply both settings to the whole catalog.
